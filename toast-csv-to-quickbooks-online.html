Toast CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Toast payments export (PaymentDetails.csv) into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Toast preset

1. Get the CSV from Toast

  1. In Toast Web, open Reports and the payments report for the days you want, then export it as CSV. With data exports set up (Reports > Settings > Data Exports), the same data arrives each night as PaymentDetails.csv.
  2. Keep Toast's default column names. Toast lets you rename and reorder export columns; the preset finds columns by name.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Toast help pages for "data exports" or "payments report".

2. What is messy about the Toast file

  • About 40 columns: Location, Payment Id, Order Id, Order #, Paid Date, ..., Amount, Tip, Gratuity, Total, ..., Refunded, Refund Date, Refund Amount, Refund Tip Amount, ..., Status, Type, ..., Card Type, ..., V/MC/D Fees.
  • Dates carry a time, like 2/1/22 10:50 AM.
  • Every attempt is a row: CAPTURED next to DENIED, VOIDED, ERROR or CANCELLED payments that never took money.
  • A refund is not its own row. It sits in the Refund columns of the original payment.
  • Cash shows Amount Tendered (what the guest handed over), not what you kept.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Toast preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the left-out note and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Toast preset does:

  • One line per payment on its Paid Date, using Total (amount plus tip and gratuity) as money in, described like Toast Credit Visa, order 12.
  • A refund becomes its own negative line (Refund Amount plus Refund Tip Amount) on the Refund Date.
  • Denied, voided, error and cancelled payments are left out and listed by row.
  • Card fees (V/MC/D Fees) are not subtracted. A note shows their total, because how Toast collects them (from each deposit or once a month) is not in the file.

Want to try it first? Download the Toast sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Upload into a Toast clearing account, not your checking account. Card deposits from Toast then become transfers you match in the bank.
  • Cash and gift card payments are in the file too. Leave them in if your clearing account tracks all sales, or untick them in QuickBooks before you import.
  • Intuit's limits for one upload: 350 KB or less and up to 1,000 lines. A busy month can pass that; export a shorter date range. The tool warns you when a file is over either limit.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
