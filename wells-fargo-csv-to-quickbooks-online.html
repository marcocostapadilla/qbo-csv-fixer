Wells Fargo CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Wells Fargo CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Wells Fargo preset

1. Download the CSV from Wells Fargo

  1. Sign in at wellsfargo.com on a desktop browser and open the account.
  2. Click Download Account Activity.
  3. Pick the date range and choose the comma delimited (spreadsheet) file format, then Download.

Bank menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Wells Fargo help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Wells Fargo file

  • There is no header row. Row 1 is already a transaction, so an importer that treats row 1 as column names silently drops your first transaction.
  • Columns are positional only: date, signed amount, an asterisk column, a check number column (usually blank) and the description.
  • Descriptions are long and packed with card numbers, reference codes and locations.
  • There is no running balance and no opening or closing figure in the file.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Wells Fargo preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Wells Fargo preset does:

  • Detects the headerless 5-column layout and keeps row 1 as a transaction.
  • Maps date, amount and description by position, ignores the asterisk column and appends the check number to the description when there is one.
  • Keeps the signed amount (money out negative) and writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY.

Want to try it first? Download the Wells Fargo sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Because the file has no balances, the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net total with your statement before importing.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
