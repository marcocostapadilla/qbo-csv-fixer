Navy Federal CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Navy Federal Credit Union CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Navy Federal preset

1. Download the CSV from Navy Federal

  1. Sign in at navyfederal.org on a computer (the mobile app does not offer the CSV export) and open the checking, savings or credit card account.
  2. Use the Download button near the Transaction History header.
  3. Pick the date range and choose CSV. Each account downloads separately.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Navy Federal help pages for "download transactions". Statements are PDF only, so use the account history download, not Statements.

2. What is messy about the Navy Federal file

  • The header is long: Posting Date, Transaction Date, Amount, Credit Debit Indicator, type, Type Group, Reference, ..., Description, Category, Check Serial Number, Card Ending.
  • Every Amount is positive. Whether money went out or came in is only in Credit Debit Indicator (Debit or Credit).
  • QuickBooks Online reads the positive amounts as money in, so every purchase would land as a deposit.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Navy Federal preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Navy Federal preset does:

  • Sets the sign from Credit Debit Indicator: Debit becomes money out (negative), Credit money in.
  • Leaves out any row whose indicator is neither Debit nor Credit and lists it, so nothing is guessed.
  • Uses Posting Date (the date on your statement) and Description; drops the other columns; removes thousands separators.

Want to try it first? Download the Navy Federal sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Card and checking downloads share the same layout, so the same preset reads both.
  • No balances in the file, so the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net with your statement.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
