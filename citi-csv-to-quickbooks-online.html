Citi CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Citi CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Citi preset

1. Download the CSV from Citi

  1. Sign in to Citi on a computer and open the credit card account.
  2. Use the Download link on the account activity page.
  3. Pick a closed statement period or a custom date range and choose CSV.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Citi help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Citi file

  • Columns: Status, Date, Description, Debit, Credit; newer files add Member Name.
  • Purchases and fees sit in Debit, payments and refunds in Credit, with the other column blank. Depending on the file, credits are written positive or with a minus sign.
  • The Status column includes Pending rows that can still change or disappear.
  • There is no opening or closing balance in the file.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Citi preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Citi preset does:

  • Turns Debit into money out (negative) and Credit into money in (positive), whichever way the credit is written.
  • Keeps Cleared rows only and lists every Pending row it left out.
  • Writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY and drops Status and Member Name.

Want to try it first? Download the Citi sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. In the free export each description ends with "(QBO CSV Fixer free)" and the file name ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. There is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Upload the result into the Citi credit card account in QuickBooks Online.
  • Pending rows are left out on purpose. Download again after they post if you need them.
  • With no balances in the file, the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net total with your statement.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
