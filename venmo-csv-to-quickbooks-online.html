Venmo CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Venmo statement CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Venmo preset

1. Download the CSV from Venmo

  1. Sign in at venmo.com and open Statements. Business profiles have their own statements.
  2. Pick the month and download the CSV.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Venmo help pages for "transaction history".

2. What is messy about the Venmo file

  • Two lines come before the header: Account Statement - (@your-username) and Account Activity.
  • The header starts with an empty column: ,ID,Datetime,Type,Status,Note,From,To,Amount (total),...,Funding Source,Destination,Beginning Balance,Ending Balance,... Business profiles add tip, tax and tax rate columns.
  • Amounts carry a sign and a space: + $650.00 is money in, - $42.50 is money out.
  • The beginning balance sits on its own line under the header, the ending balance on a line near the bottom, followed by a long disclaimer.
  • Datetime looks like 2026-03-27T10:10:10.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Venmo preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Venmo preset does:

  • Finds the header on line 3 and skips the title, balance and disclaimer lines.
  • Uses Amount (total) with its sign, and Type, Note, From and To as the description.
  • Reads Beginning Balance and Ending Balance for the reconcile badge, so a missing or doubled row shows up as FAIL before you import.
  • Writes Datetime as MM/DD/YYYY.

Want to try it first? Download the Venmo sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Amount (fee) is not subtracted again. Public samples did not show whether Amount (total) already includes a fee; if the badge fails by exactly your statement period Venmo fees, add the fees as one line in QuickBooks Online.
  • Standard Transfer rows (to your bank) are money out. Match them to the deposit in your bank account.
  • The time of day is dropped; the date is used as written in the file.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
