Etsy CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Etsy monthly statement CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Etsy preset

1. Download the CSV from Etsy

  1. In Shop Manager, open Finances, then Payment account.
  2. Find the monthly statements, pick the month and download the CSV.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Etsy help pages for "payment account" or "monthly statement".

2. What is messy about the Etsy file

  • Columns: Date, Type, Title, Info, Currency, Amount, Fees & Taxes, Net. Recent files add Tax Details, Status, Availability Date to the header.
  • One sale becomes many rows: a Sale row, then separate Fee rows (transaction fee, processing fee), Tax rows (sales tax paid by buyer), listing fees and Etsy Ads.
  • Empty money cells are written as --. Dates are text, like March 20, 2026 (older files: 16 January, 2024).
  • Refunds read Refund to buyer for Order #... and are negative, often with a positive Credit for processing fee row.
  • Deposits to your bank show the amount only in the Title, for example $12.00 sent to your bank account; every money cell on that row is --.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Etsy preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Etsy preset does:

  • Uses Net as the amount and Type, Title and Info as the description.
  • Treats -- as empty and reads a deposit amount from its Title as money out of the Etsy balance.
  • Writes text dates as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Leaves out rows whose Status is Pending (money not available yet) and lists them in the left-out note above the preview. Upload them next month, once they clear.
  • Fits an Etsy clearing account in QuickBooks Online: deposits become transfers you match in your real bank account.

Want to try it first? Download the Etsy sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • The Sale amount includes the sales tax the buyer paid, and a Tax row takes it back out. Categorize both so the tax nets to zero.
  • This statement has no opening or ending balance, so the reconcile badge shows n/a.
  • Shops paid in euro or pound get amounts like -€0.17 or £1,208.35; they are read the same way.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
