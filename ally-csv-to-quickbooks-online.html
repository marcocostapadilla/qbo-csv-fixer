Ally Bank CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Ally Bank CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Ally Bank preset

1. Download the CSV from Ally Bank

  1. Sign in at ally.com on a computer and select the account (checking, savings or money market).
  2. Scroll to Transaction History and select the download icon.
  3. Pick the date range, choose CSV and save the file. Ally downloads one account at a time.

Steps from the Ally Bank help center ("Can I download my account activity?") and export guides by Tiller and ScanCompte. Menus can change.

2. What is messy about the Ally Bank file

  • The header is Date, Time, Amount, Type, Description with a space after every comma, which some importers do not match.
  • Dates are YYYY-MM-DD; QuickBooks Online wants one US or EU format such as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • The extra Time and Type columns break the 3-column layout QuickBooks Online expects.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Ally Bank preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Ally Bank preset does:

  • Matches the spaced headers and uses Date, Description and Amount (already signed: withdrawals negative, deposits positive).
  • Writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY; drops Time and Type.
  • No balances in the file, so the reconcile badge shows N/A. A sign warning appears if the money in and out look swapped.

Want to try it first? Download the Ally Bank sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Each Ally account is a separate file, so upload each one to its own QuickBooks account.
  • Compare the net change with your Ally statement before you upload.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
