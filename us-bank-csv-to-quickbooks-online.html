U.S. Bank CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your U.S. Bank CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the U.S. Bank preset

1. Download the CSV from U.S. Bank

  1. Sign in at usbank.com on a computer (the mobile app has no transaction download).
  2. Select the account, then the download icon in the top right of the Transactions tile.
  3. Choose the date range and Spreadsheet (.CSV) as the format, then Download. Up to 18 months of history is available.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the U.S. Bank help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the U.S. Bank file

  • Columns: Date, Transaction, Name, Memo, Amount.
  • Transaction (debit or credit) and Memo are extra columns the QuickBooks Online importer does not need.
  • Amount is already signed (debits negative), so the file mostly needs trimming and consistent dates.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the U.S. Bank preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the U.S. Bank preset does:

  • Uses Name as the description and keeps the signed Amount.
  • Drops Transaction and Memo and writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Offers the 3-column (Date, Description, Amount) or 4-column (Date, Description, Debit, Credit) QuickBooks layout.

Want to try it first? Download the U.S. Bank sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. In the free export each description ends with "(QBO CSV Fixer free)" and the file name ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. There is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • The file has no balances, so the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net total with your statement.
  • Longer periods may need several downloads; the free tier handles one file of up to 100 rows.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
