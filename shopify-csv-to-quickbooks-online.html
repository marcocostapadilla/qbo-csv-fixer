Shopify Payments CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Shopify Payments payout transactions CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Shopify Payments preset

1. Download the CSV from Shopify Payments

  1. In Shopify admin, open the Shopify Payments payouts page (Settings, then Payments, then View payouts).
  2. Open a payout to export only its transactions, or stay on the list for a date range, and choose Export to get the transactions CSV.

Menus change from time to time, and depending on region the file downloads in the browser or arrives by email. If a label differs, search the Shopify help pages for "payout details".

2. What is messy about the Shopify Payments file

  • One row per charge, refund or adjustment: Transaction Date, Type, Order, Card Brand, Card Source, Payout Status, Payout Date, Available On, Amount, Fee, Net, ... Some regions add VAT or GST columns.
  • Transaction Date has a time and a UTC offset, for example 2026-03-18 10:02:11 -0400.
  • Amount is gross, Fee is separate and Net is Amount minus Fee. Refunds and adjustments are negative.
  • Rows with Payout Status pending are real charges that have not been paid out yet.
  • There is no payout ID column, and the payout to your bank is not a row in this file.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Shopify Payments preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Shopify Payments preset does:

  • Uses Net as the amount (the fee is never subtracted a second time) and Type plus Order as the description.
  • Keeps refunds and adjustments negative and keeps pending rows.
  • Drops the time and offset and writes Transaction Date as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Fits a Shopify Payments clearing account in QuickBooks Online: record each payout as a transfer to your bank and match it there.

Want to try it first? Download the Shopify Payments sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. In the free export each description ends with "(QBO CSV Fixer free)" and the file name ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. There is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • To match one bank deposit, export only that payout. The sum of Net should equal the payout amount Shopify shows.
  • This export has no opening or ending balance, so the reconcile badge shows n/a.
  • Keep each currency in its own export and its own QuickBooks account.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
