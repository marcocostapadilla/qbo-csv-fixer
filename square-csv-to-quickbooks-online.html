Square CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Square transactions CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Square preset

1. Download the CSV from Square

  1. In the Square Dashboard, open Transactions and pick the date range and location.
  2. Choose Export, then the Transactions CSV (not the item detail CSV).

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Square help pages for "export transactions".

2. What is messy about the Square file

  • About 54 columns: Date, Time, Time Zone, Gross Sales, Discounts, Service Charges, Net Sales, ..., Total Collected, ..., Fees, Net Total, Transaction ID, ..., Description, Event Type, ... Older files have fewer columns in a different order.
  • Fees are negative and written in parentheses, for example ($2.55). Net Total is what you keep after fees.
  • Refunds are their own rows with Event Type Refund and negative amounts.
  • Cash sales sit in the same file with a $0.00 fee, but that cash never reaches a Square deposit.
  • Recent exports write dates as 2026-03-06; older ones as 12/27/2018. The time is in its own column.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Square preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Square preset does:

  • Uses Net Total as the amount and Event Type plus Description as the description.
  • Reads ($33.62) as -33.62 and $1,149.00 as 1149.00.
  • Writes every date as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Keeps refunds as money out, so you can upload into a Square clearing account in QuickBooks Online and match each Square deposit to your real bank account.

Want to try it first? Download the Square sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Cash sales are included. If you do not book cash through the Square clearing account, exclude or recategorize those lines in For review.
  • This export has no opening or ending balance, so the reconcile badge shows n/a. Compare the net total with your Square sales summary instead.
  • Square deposits (transfers) come in a separate export with a different layout; this preset reads the Transactions CSV.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
