American Express CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your American Express CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the American Express preset

1. Download the CSV from American Express

  1. Sign in at americanexpress.com on a computer.
  2. Open Statements & Activity and pick a billing period or a custom date range.
  3. Choose Download, select CSV. Ticking the option to include all additional details is fine; the extra columns are ignored.

Bank menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the American Express help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the American Express file

  • The amount is written from the card's point of view: charges are positive, payments and credits are negative.
  • QuickBooks Online expects the opposite for a card account (charges negative, payments positive). Imported as is, every charge shows up as a payment.
  • Extra columns such as Card Member and Account #, plus many more if you include additional details.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the American Express preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the American Express preset does:

  • Flips every sign: a 412.80 charge becomes -412.80 and a 500.00 payment becomes 500.00.
  • Keeps Date, Description and Amount, drops the rest, writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Shows a preview so you can check two or three charges against your statement before you download.

Want to try it first? Download the American Express sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Upload the result into the American Express credit card account in QuickBooks Online, not into a bank account.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
