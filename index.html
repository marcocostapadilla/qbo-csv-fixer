Why not just paste into chat?

  • Client-data policy risk: many bookkeepers cannot paste client bank CSVs into a chatbot.
  • Silent reconcile miss: ChatGPT can return a CSV that looks import-ready while missing an opening/closing balance break (our sample fixture has a built-in $2 gap; chat did not warn).
  • No one-click local UI: chat offered DIY scripts, not a bookmarkable browser tool that never uploads the file.

The point of this tool is the visible reconcile badge plus an offline, one-click UI.
