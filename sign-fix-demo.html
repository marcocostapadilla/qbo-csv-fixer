Wrong signs, before and after

Two example files (made-up names) with the same five transactions, opening balance $1500.00 and ending balance $2087.45. The numbers below are what the tool shows for each file.

Before: every amount is a plus number

This file has one Amount column with no minus signs, so rent and bills read as money in.

Net change $4296.75. Reconcile: FAIL. Opening $1500.00 plus these rows is $5796.75, but the file's ending balance is $2087.45 (off by -$3709.30).

After: money out and money in kept apart

The same rows exported with separate Debit and Credit columns. The tool reads Debit as money out and Credit as money in.

Net change $587.45. Reconcile: PASS. Opening balance plus these rows equals the ending balance ($2087.45).

Open the after file in the tool or download it.

The tool never flips signs on its own. It reads signs the way your bank writes them: Debit and Credit columns, or a bank preset such as Navy Federal, Apple Card or American Express. When the file has opening and ending balances, a wrong sign shows up as a FAIL with the gap, as above. Without balances, a file where every amount is a plus number gets a sign warning. If your bank's file looks like the "before" file, export it again with Debit and Credit columns, or pick your bank in the list.
