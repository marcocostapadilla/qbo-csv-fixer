PayPal CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your PayPal CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the PayPal preset

1. Download the CSV from PayPal

  1. Sign in to PayPal and open Activity download (under Activity or Reports, depending on your account type).
  2. Pick the date range and CSV as the format, then create the report.
  3. Download the file when it is ready (PayPal names it Download.CSV).

Bank menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the PayPal help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the PayPal file

  • Many columns: Date, Time, TimeZone, Name, Type, Status, Currency, Gross, Fee, Net, then emails, Transaction ID and optional fields.
  • Not every row moves money. Rows with Balance Impact = Memo (for example authorizations) do not change your PayPal balance.
  • Gross, Fee and Net are separate; the amount that hits your PayPal balance is Net.
  • The date format follows your PayPal country; US accounts get MM/DD/YYYY.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the PayPal preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the PayPal preset does:

  • Uses Net as the amount (money out negative).
  • Skips Balance Impact = Memo rows, or, when that column is missing, rows whose Status is not Completed, Refunded or Reversed. The rows left out are listed.
  • Combines Type and Name into the description, writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY. If every date could be read either way, you get a US/EU date toggle.

Want to try it first? Download the PayPal sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export ends with one watermark row (blank date, amount 0.00). Untick it on the review screen or delete that last line first.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Using Net means fees are netted into each line. If you book gross sales and PayPal fees separately, this preset does not do that yet.
  • Multi-currency accounts produce currency conversion rows; filter to one currency per QuickBooks account.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
