Cash App CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Cash App CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Cash App preset

1. Download the CSV from Cash App

  1. On a computer, sign in at cash.app (the phone app has statements, not CSV).
  2. Click Statements in the upper right of the Activity page.
  3. Click Export CSV to download your transaction history.

Steps from export guides by How-To Geek, Coinpanda and Ledgible. Some accounts get the file by email instead.

2. What is messy about the Cash App file

  • The header is long: Transaction ID, Date, Transaction Type, Currency, Amount, Fee, Net Amount, Asset Type, ..., Status, Notes, Name of sender/receiver, Account.
  • Dates can look like 2023-07-16 16:51:24 PDT, with a time and time zone that QuickBooks Online cannot read.
  • Amount is before the Cash App fee; what moved your balance is Net Amount. Amounts carry $ signs.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Cash App preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Cash App preset does:

  • Uses Net Amount (after fees), already signed; removes the $ signs.
  • Keeps the calendar date and drops the time and zone; writes MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Builds the description from Transaction Type, Notes and Name. Skips failed, canceled, declined and pending rows and lists them.

Want to try it first? Download the Cash App sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Bitcoin and stock rows are in the same file; their Net Amount is the cash that moved.
  • No balances in the file, so the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net with your Cash App statement.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
