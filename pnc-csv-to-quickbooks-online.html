PNC CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your PNC CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the PNC preset

1. Download the CSV from PNC

  1. Sign in to PNC online banking and select the account.
  2. Open the account activity and choose Download, then the comma-delimited (CSV) format.
  3. Each account, including each Virtual Wallet part (Spend, Reserve, Growth), is downloaded separately.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the PNC help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the PNC file

  • Columns: Date, Description, Withdrawals, Deposits, Balance.
  • Withdrawals and Deposits are both positive numbers in separate columns, while many importers expect one signed Amount.
  • Larger amounts can carry thousands separators, for example "1,120.45".
  • Transfers between Virtual Wallet parts show up in each part's file.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the PNC preset and drop your CSV on the page. Use the PNC preset from this link or the preset list; PNC files are not auto-detected because their headers are generic.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the PNC preset does:

  • Turns Withdrawals into money out (negative) and Deposits into money in (positive).
  • Removes thousands separators, drops Balance and writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Offers the 3-column or 4-column QuickBooks layout.

Want to try it first? Download the PNC sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. In the free export each description ends with "(QBO CSV Fixer free)" and the file name ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. There is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • PNC column names are shared by many banks, so the tool does not claim a file is from PNC on its own. Use the button above, or pick PNC in the preset list; the generic bank map reads the same columns.
  • Import each Virtual Wallet part into its own QuickBooks Online account.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free core: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
