Bank of America CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Bank of America CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Bank of America preset

1. Download the CSV from Bank of America

  1. Sign in to Bank of America Online Banking and open the checking or savings account.
  2. Use the Download link above the transaction list.
  3. Pick the statement period or date range and choose the Microsoft Excel format, which saves a .csv file.

Bank menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Bank of America help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Bank of America file

  • The file does not start with a header. It opens with a summary block: Description,,Summary Amt., then Beginning balance as of, Total credits, Total debits and Ending balance as of rows, then a blank line.
  • The real header comes after that: Date, Description, Amount, Running Bal.
  • The first row under the header is another Beginning balance as of line with no amount.
  • Larger amounts are quoted with thousands separators, for example "2,500.00".
  • QuickBooks Online reads the summary block as data, so the upload fails or imports junk lines unless you delete them by hand.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Bank of America preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Bank of America preset does:

  • Skips the summary block and the in-table Beginning balance row; none of them reach the export.
  • Uses the Beginning and Ending balance from the summary for the reconcile badge: opening + transactions must equal ending, or you see FAIL with the exact difference. If the summary block was cut off, the ending balance comes from the newest Running Bal. value instead (a missing last row cannot be caught that way).
  • Removes thousands separators, drops Running Bal., writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY.

Want to try it first? Download the Bank of America sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Bank of America credit card files

Card downloads use a different layout: Posted Date, Reference Number, Payee, Address, Amount, with no summary block and no balances. The tool recognizes it as Bank of America (credit card), or open the tool with the card preset.

  • Amount is already signed: charges are negative (money out), payments and refunds positive (money in). It is exported as is.
  • Payee becomes the description; Reference Number and Address are dropped.
  • No balances in the file, so the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net with your card statement.
