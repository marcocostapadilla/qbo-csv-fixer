Fidelity CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Fidelity brokerage or Cash Management account CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Fidelity preset

1. Download the CSV from Fidelity

  1. Sign in at fidelity.com on a computer and open Accounts & Trade, then History (or Activity & Orders).
  2. Pick the account and the date range.
  3. Choose Download and save the CSV.

Steps from public export guides (cgtcalculator.io Fidelity guide). Menus can change.

2. What is messy about the Fidelity file

  • The header is Run Date, Action, Symbol, Description, Type, Price ($), Quantity, Commission ($), Fees ($), Accrued Interest ($), Amount ($), Cash Balance ($), Settlement Date; some exports add Account columns or drop Cash Balance.
  • Cash rows often say No Description; the real text (DIRECT DEPOSIT, DEBIT CARD PURCHASE, ...) is in Action.
  • Blank lines come before the header and disclaimer text and a "Date downloaded" line come after the rows.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Fidelity preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Fidelity preset does:

  • Uses Run Date and Amount ($) (already signed, net of commission and fees); writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Uses the Action text when Description is empty or says No Description, and keeps a real Description.
  • Ignores the disclaimer lines and names them in a note. When Cash Balance ($) is present, checks every row against it: PASS, or FAIL with the difference. A missing first or last row cannot be caught.

Want to try it first? Download the Fidelity sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Buys and sells are in the file as cash movements; QuickBooks sees the cash, not the shares.
  • Export one account per file and upload each to its own QuickBooks account.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
