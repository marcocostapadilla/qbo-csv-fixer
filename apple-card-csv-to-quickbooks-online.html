Apple Card CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Apple Card CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Apple Card preset

1. Download the CSV from Apple Card

  1. On iPhone, open the Wallet app, tap Apple Card, then Card Balance.
  2. Pick a monthly statement and tap Export Transactions, or under Statements tap Export Transactions and choose a date range.
  3. Choose Comma Separated Values (CSV) and save the file to Files, or send it to your computer. On a computer: card.apple.com, Statements, Export Transactions.

Steps from Apple Support article 102284 ("Download your Apple Card statements or export your transactions"). Menus can change with iOS updates.

2. What is messy about the Apple Card file

  • Columns: Transaction Date, Clearing Date, Description, Merchant, Category, Type, Amount (USD), Purchased By.
  • Purchases are positive and payments, refunds and Daily Cash are negative, the opposite of what QuickBooks Online expects.
  • Description is the long merchant string with address; Merchant is a short name.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Apple Card preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Apple Card preset does:

  • Flips every sign: purchases become money out, payments, refunds and Daily Cash money in.
  • Uses Transaction Date and Description (the line as it appears on your statement); drops the other columns.
  • Writes dates as MM/DD/YYYY and amounts with two decimals.

Want to try it first? Download the Apple Card sample CSV (fake names) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Daily Cash adjustments are exported as money in. If you track Daily Cash elsewhere, delete those lines before uploading.
  • No balances in the file, so the reconcile badge shows N/A. Compare the net with your statement.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
