Wise CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Wise CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Wise preset

1. Download the CSV from Wise

  1. On wise.com, click the currency balance you need, then the three dots, then Statements and reports.
  2. Choose a statement, pick the period and CSV as the format. Wise asks for your password to confirm.
  3. One statement covers one currency for up to 365 days.

Bank menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Wise help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Wise file

  • Header: TransferWise ID, Date, Amount, Currency, Description, Payment Reference, Running Balance, Exchange From, Exchange To, Exchange Rate, Payer Name, Payee Name, ... and more.
  • Dates are DD-MM-YYYY, while a US QuickBooks Online company usually maps MM/DD/YYYY. Days 1 to 12 are easy to swap by accident.
  • Rows are listed newest first, and currency conversions show up as their own rows.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Wise preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Wise preset does:

  • Reads DD-MM-YYYY correctly and writes MM/DD/YYYY. If any day is above 12 the order is detected; if not, you get a visible US/EU toggle that defaults to D/M for Wise.
  • Keeps the signed Amount and builds the description from Description plus Payment Reference.
  • Drops the exchange, payer/payee and card columns.

Want to try it first? Download the Wise sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Use one QuickBooks Online account per Wise currency balance; a conversion appears in both currency statements.
  • Fee handling depends on the statement options you choose at Wise. Check one fee line against your Wise app before importing.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
