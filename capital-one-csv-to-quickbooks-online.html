Capital One CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Capital One CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Capital One preset

1. Download the CSV from Capital One

  1. Sign in to your Capital One account on a computer and select the card.
  2. Choose the option to download transactions.
  3. Pick the date range and CSV as the format.

Bank menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Capital One help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Capital One file

  • Seven columns: Transaction Date, Posted Date, Card No., Description, Category, Debit, Credit.
  • Debit (purchases, fees) and Credit (payments, refunds) are both written as positive numbers, with one of them blank on each row.
  • Dates are usually YYYY-MM-DD, while a US QuickBooks Online company usually maps MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Card No. and Category are Capital One's own fields and do not belong in the import.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Capital One preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Capital One preset does:

  • Turns Debit into a negative amount and Credit into a positive amount, or keeps them as separate columns if you pick the 4-column QuickBooks layout.
  • Uses Transaction Date and converts it to MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Drops Posted Date, Card No. and Category.

Want to try it first? Download the Capital One sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Upload the result into the Capital One credit card account in QuickBooks Online.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
