Mercury CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Mercury CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Mercury preset

1. Download the CSV from Mercury

  1. In Mercury, open the Transactions page.
  2. Filter to one account and the date range you need, then choose Export filtered (or Export all).
  3. Pick CSV and Download.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Mercury help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Mercury file

  • Many columns: Date (UTC), Description, Amount, Status, Source Account, Bank Description, Reference, Note, Last Four Digits, Name On Card, Category, GL Code, Timestamp, Original Currency.
  • Status can be Sent, Pending, Failed or Cancelled; only Sent rows moved money.
  • Dates are MM-DD-YYYY in UTC, with dashes instead of slashes.
  • An export of all accounts mixes checking and credit rows, and a transfer between them appears twice.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Mercury preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Mercury preset does:

  • Keeps Sent rows only and lists every Pending, Failed or other row it left out.
  • Keeps the signed Amount and uses Description (falls back to Bank Description).
  • Reads MM-DD-YYYY and writes MM/DD/YYYY.

Want to try it first? Download the Mercury sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Export one Mercury account per file and upload it into the matching QuickBooks Online account.
  • Dates are UTC; a transaction near midnight can land on a different local day.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
