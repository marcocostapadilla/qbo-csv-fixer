Discover CSV to QuickBooks Online

Plain steps to get your Discover CSV export into QuickBooks Online through Banking > Upload from file. The fixing happens in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Open the tool with the Discover preset

1. Download the CSV from Discover

  1. Sign in at discover.com on a computer (the download is not in the mobile app).
  2. Open the card account and use the download link above the activity list.
  3. Pick a date range and choose CSV. Only activity Discover still shows online is included.

Menus change from time to time. If a label differs, search the Discover help pages for "download transactions" or "export CSV".

2. What is messy about the Discover file

  • Columns: Trans. Date, Post Date, Description, Amount, Category.
  • Purchases are positive and payments or credits are negative, the opposite of what QuickBooks Online expects for a card account.
  • Two dates per row and Discover's own Category column.

3. How QBO CSV Fixer fixes it

  1. Open QBO CSV Fixer with the Discover preset and drop your CSV on the page. The preset is also auto-detected from the file layout.
  2. Check the preview, the reconcile badge and, if shown, the date format warning.
  3. Download the QuickBooks-ready CSV.

What the Discover preset does:

  • Flips every sign: a 46.35 purchase becomes -46.35 and a -300.00 payment becomes 300.00.
  • Uses Trans. Date (falls back to Post Date) and writes MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Drops Post Date and Category.

Want to try it first? Download the Discover sample CSV (fake merchants) and drop it on the tool.

4. Upload to QuickBooks Online

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open Banking (in newer menus: Transactions, then Bank transactions).
  2. Select the account, open the Link account drop-down and choose Upload from file.
  3. Drag in the CSV you downloaded from QBO CSV Fixer and pick the QuickBooks account it belongs to.
  4. Map the columns: Date to Date, Description to Description, and Amount as one column with positive and negative values (or Debit and Credit as two columns if you exported the 4-column layout). Choose the MM/dd/yyyy date format.
  5. The free export is marked only in its file name, which ends with _qbo-csv-fixer-free. Nothing is added to the descriptions, and there is no extra watermark row to remove.
  6. Select the transactions to import, then Continue and Done. They land in For review to categorize or match.

Good to know

  • Upload the result into the Discover credit card account in QuickBooks Online, not into a bank account.
  • Check two or three purchases in the preview against your statement before you download.

Scope

CSV only. No PDF statements, no bank login, no server: the file is read and converted by JavaScript in your browser. Free version: 1 file, up to 100 rows, watermarked export. Unlimited version coming soon.
